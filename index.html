Bozo League Official Rules

  • Eligible Games: Weekly picks must be for Sunday or Monday NFL games.
  • Odds Floor Restriction: No pick can be made on odds lower than -375 (e.g., -400 or -450 is illegal).
  • Submission Deadline: Picks must be submitted by noon on Saturday.
  • Forfeiture Penalty: Failure to submit a bet by noon on Saturday results in an automatic Bozo designation for the following week. Admins can force-assign this bozo manually to bypass voting.
  • Automatic Single-Loss Rule: If exactly 1 bet loses while all other settled bets win, that sole loser automatically becomes the Bozo (no vote required).
  • Voting Process: When multiple bets lose, the Bozo is selected by blind group vote. Identities and final counts reveal once all votes are cast.
  • Role & Payout Access: Only League Admins or the current Slate Bozo can set the stake amount ($20 minimum) or update leg outcomes.
  • The Darcy Amnesty Clause: While Darcy owes the group $44,000, we forgive him and it is a completely clean slate for the season!
